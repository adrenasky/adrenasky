Notes that show the working

  • Fire classes: A = solids (paper, wood, fabric); B = liquids such as fuel and oil; electrical fires; special fires = metals, gases and chemicals.
  • Water: on class A solids only. Never on liquid, electrical or metal fires.
  • Cockpit fire: halon or CO2 only. Powder blinds the crew; water conducts.
  • Hot brakes: approach the wheels only from the front or rear, because tyres burst sideways.

Questions

About ATPL Operational Procedures

How many questions are in the exam, and what is the pass mark?

Under the East African CASSOA system, each ATPL theory paper is usually 40 questions in about one hour. The pass mark is 70%, but aim for 75% or more in practice so you have a margin on exam day.

Which Operational Procedures topics are tested most?

Fire and smoke, instruments, equipment and oxygen, operator and flight preparation, long-range and NAT operations, and all-weather minima make up over half the paper. The numbers matter: approach category speeds, RVR minima and oxygen requirements.

Does it cover the Kenyan regulations (KCARs)?

Yes. Where the KCAA question bank gives a different answer from older JAR-OPS material, the explanation says so, so you know which figure the exam expects.
