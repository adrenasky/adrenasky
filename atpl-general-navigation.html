Notes that show the working

  • Conversions: 1 NM = 1.852 km; 1 m/s ≈ 1.94 kt; 1 US gal = 3.785 L; 1 imp gal = 4.546 L.
  • Time: distance ÷ groundspeed. Work in minutes by dividing by GS/60.
  • Wind components: crosswind = wind speed × sin(angle); headwind = wind speed × cos(angle).
  • Glide path: rate of descent ≈ groundspeed × 5 for a 3° slope.

Questions

About ATPL General Navigation

How many questions are in the exam, and what is the pass mark?

Under the East African CASSOA system, each ATPL theory paper is usually 40 questions in about one hour. The pass mark is 70%, but aim for 75% or more in practice so you have a margin on exam day.

Which General Navigation topics are tested most?

The navigation computer and great circles together make up over half the paper. Speed, time, fuel and wind calculations are free marks once you have the method.

Do I need a flight computer for the exam?

Most students use a CRP-5 or similar flight computer, and the worked examples show the arithmetic behind each answer so you can check yourself. Confirm what you may bring with your examining authority.
