Notes that show the working

  • Transition: at or below the transition altitude, fly altitude on QNH. The transition level is the lowest flight level available above it.
  • MSA: at least 300 m (984 ft) obstacle clearance within 25 NM of the homing facility.
  • Holding: outbound leg 1 minute at or below 14,000 ft, 1 minute 30 seconds above.
  • Missed approach: designed on a nominal 2.5% climb gradient.
Worked example · departure climb gradientProcedure design gradient (PDG) = obstacle surface + margin.3.3% = 2.5% + 0.8%Minimum PDG 3.3% unless the chart says otherwise
Key numbers · parallel runway approachesIndependent approaches: 1,000 ft vertical or 3 NM radar separation until established on the localizer.Runways under 760 m apart count as one for wake turbulence.

Questions

About ATPL Air Law

How many questions are in the exam, and what is the pass mark?

Under the East African CASSOA system, each ATPL theory paper is usually 40 questions in about one hour. The pass mark is 70%, but aim for 75% or more in practice so you have a margin on exam day.

Which Air Law topics are tested most?

Instrument and approach procedures, rules of the air, air traffic services and ICAO's framework make up over 60% of the paper. Learn their numbers well: separation minima, holding times and MSA.

Does it cover Kenya's own regulations?

Yes. Alongside ICAO material, the Air Law notes include Kenya national regulations and KCAA rules, so you learn where Kenya differs from the ICAO standard.
