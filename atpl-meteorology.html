Notes preview · Altimetry

Notes that show the working

  • Hectopascal rule: near sea level 1 hPa ≈ 27 ft. It grows with height, to about 50 ft at FL200.
  • QFE vs QNH: at an aerodrome above sea level QFE is lower than QNH; below sea level QFE is higher.
  • Temperature error: in air colder than ISA the altimeter over-reads. Hot to cold, look out below.
  • True altitude: correct by 4 ft per 1,000 ft per 1°C ISA deviation. Add if warmer, subtract if colder.
Worked example · pressure and temperature togetherFL100, QNH 1003 hPa, OAT −15°C.1. Pressure: 10,000 − (1013 − 1003) × 27 = 9,730 ft2. Temperature: ISA −10, so 4 × 9.73 × 10 ≈ 390 ftTrue altitude ≈ 9,340 ft

About ATPL Meteorology

How many questions are in the exam, and what is the pass mark?

Under the East African CASSOA system, each ATPL theory paper is usually 40 questions in about one hour. The pass mark is 70%, but aim for 75% or more in practice so you have a margin on exam day.

Which Met topics are tested most?

Visibility, altimetry, clouds, air masses and pressure systems carry the most questions. Altimetry calculations come up in almost every paper, so practise them until they are automatic.

Is Adrenasky useful for KCAA exams?

Yes. The question banks follow the ATPL syllabus the KCAA exams are based on, and the notes are written for students training in East Africa.
